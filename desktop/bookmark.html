从 X 到你的知识库
One click to Obsidian.

把下面的按钮拖到浏览器书签栏，只需设置一次。之后在任意 X 帖子详情页点击它，即可保存正文并打开 Obsidian。

Drag the button into your browser's bookmarks bar once. On an X post, click that bookmark to save the content and open Obsidian.

保存到 Obsidian / Save to Obsidian
  1. 显示 Chrome 书签栏：按 Command + Shift + B。Show Chrome's bookmarks bar with Command + Shift + B.
  2. 将紫色按钮拖到书签栏。Drag the purple button onto the bookmarks bar.
  3. 在 X 打开具体帖子，点击书签；首次提示时允许打开 X to Obsidian。Open a specific X post and click the bookmark. Allow the app to open when prompted the first time.

也可以打开 “X to Obsidian” 应用并粘贴帖子链接。不需要终端命令。You can also paste a post link into the X to Obsidian app. No Terminal commands are needed.
